# Ambition vs Entitlement

## Key Distinction
- **Ambition** is expecting **yourself** to close the gap between what you have and what you want.
- **Entitlement** is expecting **others** to do it for you.

## Understanding True Motivation

Many of our ambitions are just insecurities. Our lives are often driven by shame—not true ambition. It usually takes failure to see the illusions we've been chasing.

**"Some people need to prove to the world that they're worthy."**


## Taking Responsibility

True ambition involves:
- Taking full ownership of your outcomes
- Not waiting for external validation or assistance
- Focusing on what you can control
- Building trust with yourself through action
