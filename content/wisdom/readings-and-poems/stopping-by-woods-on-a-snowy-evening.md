# Stopping by Woods on a Snowy Evening

*Robert Frost*

## Selected excerpt

> The woods are lovely, dark and deep,  
> But I have promises to keep,  
> And miles to go before I sleep.

## Why it matters

The force is in the tension: beauty invites surrender, but commitments keep the speaker moving.
