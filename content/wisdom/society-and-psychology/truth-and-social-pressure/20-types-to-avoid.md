# 20 Types to Avoid

1. Always complaining about everything.
2. Loves drama and thrives in chaos.
3. Only reaches out when they need something.
4. Never takes responsibility for their mistakes.
5. Manipulates situations to get their way.
6. Always makes it about them, never about you.
7. Can't handle your success or happiness.
8. Pretends to be someone they're not.
9. Sees the worst in every situation.
10. Can't keep a secret to save their life.
11. Always has something negative to say about you.
12. Doesn't celebrate your wins, only their own.
13. Bails out when you need them the most.
14. Feels threatened by anyone else's success.
15. Lies about everything, big and small.
16. Doesn't make any effort to stay connected.
17. Constantly plays the victim in every scenario.
18. Judges you for every little thing you do.
19. Always taking, but never giving back.
20. Makes bad choices that hold them back every time.
