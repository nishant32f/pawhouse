# Ambition with AI Models

Source: [How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models](https://felixrieseberg.com/how-to-be-more-ambitious-a-tutorial-for-humans-with-models/) — **Felix Rieseberg**, 13 October 2026.

## Practical takeaway:

Use AI to rethink the objective and method, not merely accelerate an existing task. Give it the tools, data, and independent checks needed to deliver a useful outcome.

## Article summary

Rieseberg separates ambition into four dimensions:

- **Speed:** complete familiar work faster.
- **Altitude:** move from producing an artifact to achieving its underlying purpose. A quarterly deck becomes a system for detecting problems and improving decisions.
- **Depth:** use expert methods on raw evidence. Test assumptions, seek new signals, and validate against something beyond the model's own judgment.
- **Range:** borrow techniques from other disciplines. Seek their investigative methods, not theatrical role-play.

Higher-level delegation requires infrastructure: access to relevant data, software, and environments for testing. His crash-fixing example depends on connecting reports to code versions and reproducing failures across platforms.

## Startup application

An application of the framework, rather than a quotation from the article:

Before delegating a project, write down:

1. The business outcome and the decision it should enable.
2. The evidence the agent may access and the boundaries it must respect.
3. The methods worth testing, including those outside your usual discipline.
4. An acceptance test that can fail independently of a persuasive explanation.

Start with a reversible trial. Broader ambition should increase the quality of the experiment, not remove accountability.
