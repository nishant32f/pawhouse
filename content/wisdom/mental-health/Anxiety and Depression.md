# Anxiety and Depression

## Critical Self-Assessment Questions
### Depression vs Life Circumstances
**Am I depressed or is my life depressing?**

This question helps distinguish between clinical depression and situational unhappiness. Sometimes what feels like depression might be a natural response to genuinely difficult life circumstances.

### Anxiety vs Rational Concern
**Am I anxious or would any normal person find my life anxiety-inducing?**

This helps differentiate between anxiety disorders and reasonable stress responses to genuinely stressful situations.

### Social Issues vs Personal Issues
**Am I a loner or are the people around me not supportive?**

This distinguishes between personal social difficulties and being in an unsupportive environment.
