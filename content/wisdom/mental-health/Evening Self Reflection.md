# Evening Self Reflection

A daily practice for developing emotional awareness and processing the day's experiences.

## The Five Questions

1. **What am I really worried about?**
   - Helps identify genuine concerns vs unnecessary anxiety
   - Distinguishes between actionable worries and rumination

2. **What am I presently sad about?**
   - Acknowledges and validates emotional experiences
   - Prevents emotional suppression and builds awareness

3. **Who has annoyed me and how?**
   - Processes interpersonal friction
   - Helps identify patterns in relationships and triggers

4. **What does my body want?**
   - Connects with physical needs and sensations
   - Bridges mind-body awareness

5. **What is still lovely?**
   - Cultivates gratitude and appreciation
   - Balances negative focus with positive recognition
   - Develops appreciation of small joys
