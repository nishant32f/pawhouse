# Personal Beliefs & Life Principles

These are beliefs and principles I've collected over the years. Some from experience, some from failure, most from both.

- Take care of the people who work with you, and the work will take care of itself
- Know thyself is the greatest purpose of life
- Meaning matters more than happiness
- I know nothing
- I don't blame anything or anyone for my failures or bad decisions. Only myself
- Discipline is the highest form of self-love
- Our greatest teachers come disguised as difficulties
- Deep understanding is the foundation of all effective problem-solving
- Real wealth is having absolutely no health problem.
- Skills to be cultivated for greatness
    - Showing up despite when you don't feel like
    - Self reliance
    - Emotional Intelligence
