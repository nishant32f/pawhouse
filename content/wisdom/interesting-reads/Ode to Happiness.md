# Ode to Happiness


I DRAW A HOT SORROW BATH IN MY DESPAIR ROOM 
WITH A MISERY CANDLE BURNING 
I WASH MY HAIR WITH REGRET SHAMPOO 
AFTER CLEANING MYSELF WITH PAIN SOAP 
I DRY MYSELF WITH MY GORGEOUS WHITE ONE HUNDRED PERCENT AND
IT WILL NEVER CHANGE TOWEL 
THEN SMOOTH ON MY I DON’T DESERVE LOTION AND 
I HATE MYSELF FACE CREAM 
THEN I PUT ON MY ALONE AGAIN SILK PYJAMAS AND 
GO TO SLEEP WHEN THE HUE HAS GONE BLUE AND
YOU CAN’T QUITE GRIN AND 
BEAR IT LET THIS WORD PICTURE REMIND YOU 
IT CAN ALWAYS BE WORSE

By Keanu Reeves
