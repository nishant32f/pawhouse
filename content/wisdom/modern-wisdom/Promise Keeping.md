# Promise Keeping

## Building Self-Trust

Start with action. Make small promises to yourself—and keep them.

**If you had a friend who constantly broke plans, you'd stop trusting them. That's the relationship many of us have with ourselves.** We don't trust our own word, because we often don't follow through. Life is convenient, and it's easy not to stick to our goals.

## Starting Small

**Start by not breaking promises to yourself.** If you say you'll wake at 7 AM—do it. One win, first thing.


## The Foundation

**Do something you can feel proud of.** Ask yourself: _What would make me proud of this week?_


## Connection to Action

The promises that matter are action-based commitments, not preparation commitments.

## Why It Matters

Your relationship with yourself sets the foundation for building confidence and maintaining mental clarity.
