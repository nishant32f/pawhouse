# Time-Sensitive Opportunities

## Windows of Opportunity
**"There are certain experiences you can only have at certain times in your life."**

**"There are things you could do in your 20s for your business or your body that you simply can't do later without being irresponsible."**

**"As you get older, you might have more financial freedom but fewer physical or time freedoms."**

## The Importance of Timing

Some opportunities for extreme effort have natural expiration dates:

- Physical peak performance windows
- Early career growth phases
- Periods before major life responsibilities
- Learning capacity optimization periods
