This Three Minute Monday note preserves the durable ideas from the email and strips the newsletter machinery: promos, sponsors, upcoming podcasts, buttons, and footer noise.

## Earned Intuition

> “The young man knows the rules, but the old man knows the exceptions.” — [Oliver Wendell Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oliver_Wendell_Holmes_Jr.)

Intuition is not the opposite of discipline. It is what discipline becomes after enough repetitions. Beginners need rules because they do not yet have taste. Experts can make exceptions because they have internalised the pattern behind the rule.

## Marriage as Conversation

Nietzsche's test for marriage is brutally simple: can you keep having good conversations with this person into old age?

> “Everything else in marriage is transitory, but most of the time in interaction is spent in conversation.”

Conversation is not decoration on top of a relationship. It is the medium through which conflict, boredom, ambition, grief, logistics, children, money, aging, and ordinary days get metabolised.

Related note: [[wisdom/relationships-and-friendship/marriage-as-conversation|Marriage as Conversation]]

## Text Your Friends

When a friend crosses your mind, treat that as a prompt to reach out.

Friendship often decays from logistical drift, not dramatic betrayal. A short message is a small proof that the relationship is still alive between scheduled catch-ups, birthdays, and emergencies.

Related note: [[wisdom/relationships-and-friendship/text-your-friends|Text Your Friends]]

## Modern Fatherhood

Modern dads spend far more time on childcare than previous generations. The role has shifted from distant provider toward active daily participant: more present, more tired, and often more satisfied.

The trade is not simply loss. A life can become heavier because it contains more of what matters.

Related note: [[wisdom/relationships-and-friendship/modern-fatherhood|Modern Fatherhood]]

## What We Can Learn

- Earn the right to improvise by first mastering fundamentals.
- Choose relationships by the quality of daily interaction, not only peak chemistry.
- Lower the bar for maintaining friendship; warmth matters more than performance.
- Do not mistake meaningful load for a broken life.
