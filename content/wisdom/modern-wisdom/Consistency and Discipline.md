# Consistency and Discipline

## Life Movie Perspective
If my life were a movie, I'd want someone watching it to feel inspired—not just to succeed, but to embrace _both_ pride and shame, _both_ fear and excitement.

Because real life is lived in the highs and lows.

## Building Discipline

Discipline emerges from the daily practice of keeping small commitments to yourself. It's built through:

- Choosing difficult tasks regularly
- Maintaining consistency in small actions
- Actually executing rather than just planning

## The Foundation of Growth

Consistency and discipline create the foundation for:
- Navigating difficult transitions
- Maintaining emotional regulation during stress
- Building capacity for intense periods when needed
- Developing high personal standards

## Daily Practice

Consistency and discipline are built through daily choices:
- Keeping promises to yourself
- Choosing discomfort over comfort
- Focusing on action over preparation
- Maintaining standards regardless of external circumstances
