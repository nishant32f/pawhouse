Engineering leader and founder · Bengaluru

I'm Nishant. I've spent 17 years building large platforms, business software and AI products, and the engineering organisations that deliver them. At Flipkart and Myntra, I grew from an engineer into a leader of teams and managers. My founder work brings together commerce and AI. I care about understanding the problem, taking care of the people doing the work, and building systems customers can trust.

## Selected work

- [Spectrum](https://getspectrum.ai) brings growth tools and AI agents into a brand's existing Shopify store.
- Tectonic brought the shopper experience, experimentation and analytics together in a custom storefront platform.
- At Myntra, fashion intelligence connected trend discovery to decisions about what to design and sell.
- At Flipkart, I led catalog and retail accounting systems that supported the marketplace's scale and operations.

## Career

### Spectrum, formerly Tectonic

Growth platform for Shopify brands · Bengaluru

#### Co-founder and Chief Technology Officer

<p class="profile-date">July 2023 to present</p>

Company of up to 38 people. Engineering of up to 20 across platform and delivery teams. Approximately $3.5M raised.

- Co-founded Tectonic to make growth systematic for Shopify brands held back by slow storefronts, fragmented analytics and too many apps.
- Built custom storefronts that unified the shopper experience and analytics, replaced fragmented apps, and enabled experimentation and personalisation.
- Reached approximately 15 brands and $1M in contracted annual recurring revenue. Revenue per session rose 10 to 40% in A/B tests against the brands' own Shopify stores.
- In late 2025, pivoted to Spectrum to improve brands' existing Shopify storefronts, with AI agents doing the work for merchants.
- Built an app bundle for search, recommendations, reviews, promotions, rewards, galleries and shoppable videos. A Chief of Staff agent directs specialists that run and improve these capabilities. Specialists handle discovery, merchandising, analytics, conversion, coding and search visibility.
- Built durable workflows for large catalogs, repeatable playbooks and approval gates on consequential actions. Agent-written code runs in isolation, with custom skills, tools and workflows added where needed.
- Rebuilt the software development lifecycle around AI coding agents. Enforced rules, review and feedback loops helped a small team deliver predictably.
- Took Spectrum to approximately $370K in annual recurring revenue across subscriptions and managed growth services.
- Across both products, grew engineering from 4 to approximately 20 and scaled hiring and training.
- Worked directly with brands on requirements discovery and sales conversations, including at industry conferences.

## Principles

- Trust starts with reliability. Customers entrust us with critical parts of their business. Our software and our presence need to earn that trust.
- Pay attention to customers. Watch what is happening in their stores and respond to what they need.
- Take care of the people who work with you, and the work will take care of itself.
- I hire for ownership, grit, enthusiasm and learnability. I value people who keep learning, in technology or outside it.
- Understand the problem before writing code. Research and prototypes help test the idea before committing to it.
- Humans decide, review and own production, even when agents write the code. Failures should be visible, and claims should match what we have proven.
