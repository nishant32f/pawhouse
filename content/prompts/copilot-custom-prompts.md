# Copilot custom prompts

Thirteen prompt files for the Obsidian Copilot plugin. Each file keeps the plugin's own front matter and uses `{}` as the placeholder for the selected text. The files are stored unedited in the `copilot-custom-prompts` folder next to this note.

- [[summarize|Summarize]]. Bullet-point summary with one key point per bullet.
- [[simplify|Simplify]]. Rewrites at a 6th-grade reading level.
- [[explain-like-i-am-5|Explain like I am 5]]. Simple vocabulary and analogies for a 5-year-old.
- [[fix-grammar-and-spelling|Fix grammar and spelling]]. Corrects errors without changing content or formatting.
- [[make-longer|Make longer]]. Expands to twice the length with details and examples.
- [[make-shorter|Make shorter]]. Cuts to half the length while keeping main ideas and tone.
- [[emojify|Emojify]]. Adds emojis at natural breaks and leaves the text unchanged.
- [[remove-urls|Remove URLs]]. Strips all URLs and keeps the rest.
- [[generate-glossary|Generate glossary]]. Alphabetical "Term: Definition" list of important terms.
- [[generate-table-of-contents|Generate table of contents]]. Hierarchical table of contents with heading levels.
- [[translate-to-chinese|Translate to Chinese]]. Keeps meaning, tone, cultural context and structure.
- [[rewrite-as-tweet|Rewrite as tweet]]. A single tweet of at most 280 characters.
- [[rewrite-as-tweet-thread|Rewrite as tweet thread]]. A thread marked THREAD START and THREAD END, with each tweet under 240 characters.
