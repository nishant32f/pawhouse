# Happiness needs enjoyment, satisfaction and meaning

Arthur Brooks defines happiness as three macronutrients.

1. **Enjoyment.** Not mere pleasure. It is pleasure plus people plus memory.
2. **Satisfaction.** Achieving worthwhile goals through struggle.
3. **Meaning.** Coherence, purpose and significance. See [[meaning-is-coherence-purpose-significance|Meaning has three parts: coherence, purpose, significance]].

He argues that enjoyment and satisfaction have not collapsed the way meaning has. Strivers often have plenty of satisfaction and very little enjoyment, because they are addicted to the satisfaction of achievement and keep putting points on the board. That is the pattern in [[frankls-inverse-law|People who can't feel pleasure hide in meaning and delayed gratification]].

The remedy Brooks gives is leisure done for its own sake, covered in [[optimised-leisure-becomes-work|Optimising rest turns leisure into work]].
