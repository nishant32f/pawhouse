# Postponing life to buy time later makes the postponement permanent

Many people trade time for money so they can eventually buy time back. They trade happiness for success so they can eventually enjoy happiness. The risk is that postponement becomes a habit. You reach the future and keep postponing.

The only life you directly experience is the present one. Future success is worth something only if you are still able to inhabit it when it arrives.

The mind tends to treat the present as a means and the future as the end. It plans how to be happy later and misses the life happening now. The present can feel boring, painful or incomplete, which is why people avoid it. Constant avoidance turns even beautiful moments into background.

A test is to ask whether a goal, schedule, routine or ambition is deepening your life or helping you avoid being here.
