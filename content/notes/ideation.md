# Good startup ideas come from living in the future

When a startup launches, some users must urgently need what it makes. People who could only imagine using it someday are not enough. That first group is usually small. If many people urgently needed something that a startup could build in a version one, it would probably already exist.

Live in the future, then build what is missing. A way to notice ideas is to work on projects that seem cool. You will tend to build things that do not exist yet, because building what already exists is less interesting.

To find the first users, ask what kind of people would treat the app as a must-have. They need the motivation, the ability and the trigger.

## Parts of an idea

A startup idea has three parts.

- **Problem.** The initial conditions, meaning the setting that lets the company grow quickly.
- **Solution.** The experiment run within those conditions.
- **Insight.** The explanation for why the experiment should succeed.

## Good problems

A good problem is popular, urgent, expensive, mandatory and frequent.
