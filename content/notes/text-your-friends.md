# Text a friend when they cross your mind

When you think of a friend, use the thought as a cue to reach out.

Friendships fade less from conflict than from unexpressed affection and logistical drift. You think about people more often than they know, and a short message is one of the cheapest ways to make someone feel remembered. It can be short. "I saw this and thought of you," "miss you," "hope your week is less insane," or "this made me laugh" will do.

Small signals add up. They show the relationship is alive between catch-ups, birthdays and emergencies.

- Treat remembering someone as a prompt to act.
- Lower the bar. A short, warm message is enough.
- Send the text while the feeling is fresh.
- Maintain with small recurring signals before the relationship needs repair.
