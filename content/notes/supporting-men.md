# Men need to hear both "you can be more" and "you are enough"

Men want to aim high without feeling insufficient if they fall short. They want their suffering recognised without being pandered to or patronised, and they want to open up without being judged. They want support without feeling broken and to believe they can be more without feeling they are not enough already. They want to be loved for who they are rather than for what they do.

The difficulty is blending inspiration with compassion. The question many men carry is "How do I set lofty goals which drive me to fulfil my potential without feeling less than if I don't get there tomorrow?" Self-love and high performance conflict inside everyone, men especially.

Some men are all drive with no introspection, and some are all reflection with few outward desires. Most want a mix of encouraged self-belief and understanding support, and the two will clash.

What most men want to hear is "I know you can be more, but you are enough already and even if you just stay where you are, I'll be right here next to you. You're going to be great, but you don't need to be great, and I'm with you no matter what."

Sturgill Simpson's mother put it this way. "Boy I don't care if you hit it big because you're already number one."
