# Experienced astrologers did no better than chance

Spencer Greenberg, a Modern Wisdom guest, ran a study to test whether astrologers can show real skill. A basic claim of astrology is that a natal chart holds information about a person's life and character. If so, astrologers should pick a person's chart at a rate well above chance.

## Design

- Six astrologers helped design the study.
- In each round an astrologer got answers to 43 questions from a real person and 5 full charts. One was the person's real natal chart and 4 were decoys.
- Each astrologer made 12 matches. Random guessing gives 20 percent, about 2.4 correct out of 12.
- The results are based on 152 astrologer participants with prior experience, who predicted they would beat random guessing.
- Getting 23 percent right would have counted as a result in favour of astrology.

## Results

- As a group, astrologers got 20 percent right, indistinguishable from random guessing.
- Experience made no difference. More experienced astrologers did no better than less experienced ones, yet were far more confident. After finishing, the least experienced thought they had got 5 of 12 and the most experienced thought 10 of 12.
- The study offered a $1000 prize for 11 or more of 12. More than half believed afterwards that they had got 6 or more, but no astrologer got more than 5.
- Astrologers did not agree with each other either. Random picking would give 20 percent agreement and even the most experienced agreed only 28 percent of the time.

Despite believing they could do it, the 152 astrologers seemed to lack any ability to match people to their charts.
