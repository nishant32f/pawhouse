# Relationships are decided in the lows

Good times predict poorly how a couple will handle bad times, and handling bad times is what matters most. More relationships end from a surplus of bad events than from a scarcity of good ones.

## What to look for in a partner

- High conscientiousness and agreeableness.
- Moderate openness to experience, and not a high level of it.
- Whether you can be fully yourself around them.

Choose carefully, because you are also choosing your child's mother or father.

## Eight red flags

1. They do not understand how difficult they are to live with.
2. They label any criticism as rude or offensive.
3. They apologise repeatedly and do not change.
4. They flirt with others and dismiss your discomfort.
5. They often tell you that you are imagining things.
6. They do not value your love as a substantial gift.
7. They are too much in pain to want the best for you.
8. They deflect criticism by pointing out your imperfections.
