# Healthy minds filter, forgive, keep perspective and rank worries

Healthy minds show these traits, which support resilience, clarity, connection and purpose.

- Selective attention. Filters out distressing or irrelevant thoughts.
- A self-compassionate inner voice, with a friend's kindness.
- Resistance to unfair comparison. Others' achievements do not diminish self-worth.
- Managing fear. Separates real from hypothetical threats and avoids catastrophe spirals.
- Compartmentalisation. Keeps intrusive thoughts out of the present moment.
- Emotional presence. Silences inner noise to engage with people and surroundings.
- Balanced trust. Sceptical of some people, open to connection overall.
- Capacity for hope. Holds reasons to endure even when despair is justified.
- Appreciation of small joys.
- Emotional regulation. Does not feel everything at all times, and can delay, suppress or prioritise emotions.

Unhealthy minds show these:

- Intrusive, unfiltered thoughts without control or purpose.
- Persistently unkind inner voices that damage self-worth.
- Anxiety, with worries arriving regardless of likelihood or relevance.
- Regret, with past mistakes dominating the present and hard to forgive.
- Distorted perspective, where minor setbacks feel catastrophic.
- No ranking of concerns, so everything feels equally urgent and action is hard.
- Persistent sadness and shame, with feelings of unlovability and failure.
- Mental disorganisation, with disturbing thoughts at inappropriate times.
- Fear of losing control, such as obsessive thoughts of shouting in public or self-harm that feel close to action.
- Blurring of inner and outer reality, with others misread as extensions of one's mind.
- Nighttime despair, when exhaustion and rumination peak and suicidal thoughts feel more reasonable.
