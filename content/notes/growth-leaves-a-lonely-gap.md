# Growth leaves a lonely gap between old and new belonging

Growth can open a gap between the identity you had and the one you are building. You may leave old habits and relationships before new ones show up. The period in between is lonely and produces self-doubt.

That doubt does not automatically mean you made the wrong decision. It may be the cost of becoming different.

Difficulty and loss of belonging can be part of the price of a worthwhile path. Meaningful work usually costs sleep, certainty, energy, comfort and sometimes social belonging, and many people stop at that price. A hard path is not therefore a wrong one. The difficulty may be the cost of the path.
