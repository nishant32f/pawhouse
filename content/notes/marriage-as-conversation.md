# Choose a partner by the quality of conversation

Nietzsche's test for marriage asks whether the person will stay a good conversation partner across a life.

> "Marriage as a long conversation.
>
> When entering a marriage, one should ask the question: do you think you will be able to have good conversations with this woman right into old age?
>
> Everything else in marriage is transitory, but most of the time in interaction is spent in conversation."

Romance, status, novelty, sex, logistics and circumstances all change. Conversation is the daily medium. It is where a couple works through conflict, boredom, ambition, grief, plans, money, children, aging and ordinary Tuesday evenings. A relationship with talk that stays alive, curious, forgiving and interesting when nothing dramatic is happening has a stronger base than chemistry gives.

In practice:

- Choose for the quality of daily interaction over peak intensity.
- Notice whether disagreement turns into curiosity or contempt.
- Ask whether you can imagine being interested in this person's inner world decades from now.

## The heart decides

This test does not replace feeling. Williamson says "For most of the important decisions in life, your heart decides and head then makes it happen, not the other way round." The head models trade-offs and often rationalises an orientation that has already formed. A good decision takes account of feeling without letting vague feeling excuse sloppy thinking.
