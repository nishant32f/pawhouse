# Novelty lengthens remembered time and routine shrinks it

Present time passes at the same speed. Remembered time expands or contracts with memory density.

Routine compresses life in memory and novelty expands it. If your days are forgettable, your years will feel as though they disappeared. Giving your brain reasons to pay attention is how a life becomes memorable.

Compulsive habits work against this. Studies show that perception of time speeds up while using social media, so the hours it takes from leisure also make life feel faster (see [[parkinsons-law|Work expands to fill the time available]]).
