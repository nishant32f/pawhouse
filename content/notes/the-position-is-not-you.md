# Better treatment from a position belongs to the position

Simon Sinek says that as you gain position, people will treat you better. None of it is for you. It is for the position, the level of leader you have reached.

Be grateful for it, but remember that it is not for you. This is the leadership version of [[self-worth-beyond-validation|Self-worth outsourced to approval is never secure]].
