# Pain can drive early effort but harms you if you never outgrow it

Fear, insecurity, resentment and pain can be strong early fuel. Many people are first driven more by what they run from than by what they move toward. That fuel works. It also corrodes you if you never mature past it.

Success does not become more legitimate because you suffered more for it. High standards can become a trap where success is the minimum acceptable result and anything less counts as failure. Insecure overachievers are the usual case, and [[worry-gets-false-credit|Worry takes credit for results it did not produce]] describes how that belief protects itself.

Some people need to learn discipline. Others need to learn peace. "Work harder" is not a universal cure, a point developed in [[advice-amplifies-what-you-are|Advice amplifies what you already are]].

Questions to ask before taking on a discomfort:

- Is this difficulty meaningful?
- Is it making me more capable or more compulsive?
- Am I choosing a hard thing or using pain to prove my worth?
- What behaviour should this adversity improve?
