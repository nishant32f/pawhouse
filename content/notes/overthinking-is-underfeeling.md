# Overthinking is often underfeeling

You cannot think your way out of a feeling problem. Trying to think your way into feeling emotions is like trying to drink your way sober. Trying to think your way out of overthinking is like trying to sniff your way out of a cocaine addiction.

Gurwinder Bhogal: "Overthinking invents more problems than it solves."

Rumination can serve you. The brain gets something out of it, and finding how it serves you is the first step to stopping it. One payoff is a false sense of certainty, described in [[the-brain-prefers-catastrophe-to-uncertainty|The brain prefers an imagined catastrophe to not knowing]].

- People are never paralysed by overthinking good outcomes, only terrible ones.
- Fear does not keep you safe. It restricts you, because whatever you fear limits where you will go. Fear of heights keeps you low. Fear of people keeps you alone.
- Most people do not think about things enough. You are probably not one of them.
- Reflection and personal development have costs as well as benefits. You need self-assessment to know what to work on, but intense self-assessment restricts quality of life. The more you think about yourself, the harder happiness is to reach.
- The amount you overthink is inverse to how much you live. Do not trade the thing you want for the thing meant to help you get it.

Rest can be over-thought too, so that worry replaces the experience of being inside it. And some problems are unprocessed feelings that need safe contact. Better logic does not help with them. That is the subject of [[healing-needs-safety-and-feeling|Healing needs safety plus contact with emotion]].
