# Mental toughness has four parts: tolerance, fortitude, resilience, adaptability

Mental toughness breaks into four capacities:

- Tolerance is how much hardship you can experience before your behaviour changes.
- Fortitude is how drastic the behaviour change is once you are affected.
- Resilience is how quickly you return to baseline.
- Adaptability is whether the new baseline is better, worse or unchanged.

The goal is not to feel nothing. It is to keep your values and behaviour intact despite feeling something.

Splitting it this way shows where a given person is weak. Someone can tolerate a lot, change sharply once affected, and recover slowly. The parts can be trained separately.

The same trait does not transfer to every domain. Tolerance helps in work and training. In relationships it can turn into an inability to notice a limit, as covered in [[carrying-everything-becomes-self-abandonment|Carrying everyone else's load becomes self-abandonment]].
