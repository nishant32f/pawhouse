# Many people want comfort more than success

This is a comment by jblow, posted on 23 November 2017 under a thread titled "Resistance to Being Productive."

As you succeed in a field and take on the habits it needs, you notice many of them are easy. People just do not want to do them. So many people do not want to be successful, and would see it if they looked inward. What they want is to be somewhere comfortable in the middle of the herd, without much work.

Most people want comfort over the kind of success that requires ambition. Many have been talked into the rhetoric of success enough that they do not notice it is not what they want.

jblow adds a puzzle he had not solved. Whenever he gives advice, some people reply with self-defeating reasons it cannot apply to them. They build belief structures that keep them stuck, and defend them hard, though it would obviously be better to drop them. His tentative explanation is that if a solution exists, then not having solved the problem is your fault, and any attempt would produce a clear failure that is also your fault.
