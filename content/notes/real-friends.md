# Real friends are the people you filter yourself least around

A friend was asked who his best friend is and said he was unsure, since that is a rare question after age 12. So it was asked differently. "Who do you have the least amount of filter with when you're around them?" Another version is who you can sit in silence with without having to fill it.

Even if no single person wins, those are the people to prioritise.

We feel a compulsion to change ourselves to fit in. We adjust behaviour, words and nature to be liked, validated and accepted. The more people who make it safe to be yourself, the more confidence you have to be that person every day.

## Be selective

Adult friendship is less about small talk and plans. It involves sharing each other's silences, heartbreaks and recovery, and telling each other the truth when a lie would be easier. It depends on how well you understand each other more than on how much fun you have. It belongs to the person who stays when the room goes quiet. It means choosing each other when life is messy and inconvenient. That kind of friendship is rare, so protect it.
