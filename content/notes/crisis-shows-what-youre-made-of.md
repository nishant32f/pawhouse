# Only a crisis shows what you are made of

Chris Williamson spoke with someone who had come very close to losing everything, financially, reputationally and psychologically, many years ago. Asked how he handled the worst period, the man said he had always worried that deep down he might be a coward. He feared he was not the strong person he thought he was and would fail when it counted.

Most challenges are largely chosen, such as the job, the house, the partner and the weight lifted. They can be hard, but you chose the kind of difficulty. When everything falls apart and the worst scenario you can imagine happens, all your forces go into one challenge, and you find out what you are made of.

The man said he had faith in his ultimate self but had never been pushed hard enough to know the faith was justified. "I could always hear my best self clearing his throat in the room next door." It turned out his best self could act when needed.

Williamson says many of us are unsure how capable we are. A couple of times in a life, everything may go wrong, and your best self has to show up.
