# Carrying everyone else's load becomes self-abandonment

Psychological strength does not transfer to every domain. In training and work, enduring discomfort toward a chosen outcome is an advantage. In relationships the same trait can turn into self-abandonment, because tolerance gets mistaken for love. A relationship is not a marathon to be endured. If you pride yourself on tolerating anything, you may stay too long where attunement matters more than endurance.

The useful questions, from the 1100th Modern Wisdom episode:

- Can I handle this?
- How much do I want to tolerate?
- What does tolerating this turn me into?
- Is this strength, or an inability to feel the boundary?

If you cannot feel your emotional limit, you cannot enforce a boundary. Strength without attunement becomes self-abandonment.

## The Atlas complex

Chris Williamson's end of year lessons for 2025 ask why it is your fault when you mess up and also when other people do.

- If you care too much about harmony, you become the scapegoat in every room. The world accepts the bargain, and if you carry other people's burdens, they hand you theirs.
- There is a difference between being kind and seeming kind.
- Self-esteem cannot grow if every bruise from the world is mistaken for a self-inflicted wound.
- If everything is your fault, nothing is anyone else's.
- Boundaries are not walls. They are conditions love needs in order to survive.
- Bravery is saying "This one's not on me." Refuse to absorb other people's failures to keep the peace.
