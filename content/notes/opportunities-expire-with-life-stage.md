# Some opportunities expire with your life stage

There are experiences you can only have at certain times in your life. In your 20s there are things you can do for your business or your body that you cannot do later without being irresponsible.

As you get older, you may gain financial freedom and lose physical and time freedom.

Windows for extreme effort that close include:

- physical peak performance
- early career growth phases
- the period before major life responsibilities
- periods when learning capacity is highest

Using a window means applying maximum effort while it is open. See [[consistency-is-a-competitive-edge|Consistency is rare enough to be an edge]] for what to apply when it matters most.
