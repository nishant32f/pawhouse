# Excellence is the capacity to take pain

Travis Kalanick, talking with David Senra, says "Excellence is the capacity to take pain". In his telling, entrepreneurship is adversity and overcoming, and founders take pride in enduring more than the other side. He compares it to a world-class marathoner late in the race. If you are not pushing into pain, someone else will, and their push becomes the standard.

Alex Hormozi makes the same link to income. A $100K a year problem takes $100K worth of pain tolerance, a $1M problem takes $1M worth and a $10M problem takes $10M worth. The kind of pain changes (ignorance, then judgment, then lawsuits, then uncertainty) but it does not go away.

## Two failure modes

- **Fragility.** Avoiding hard things because they hurt.
- **Numbness.** Becoming so used to adversity that wrong things stop provoking urgency. Kalanick says there is power in being bothered by what is wrong, because that fuels fierceness.

His later target is "fierceness with calm on the inside". The founder stays bothered and intense enough to act, without internal chaos.

Pain is useful when it builds capacity and dangerous when it becomes an identity. It is a signal and a price. Capacity to absorb it still has to match the rate at which you create problems, as covered in [[problem-solving-capacity|Problem-solving capacity must keep up with problem creation]].
