# A family frees you from status games

"Fuck-you money" is an amount of wealth at which conventions and gatekeepers stop applying to you. You do not need to flatter anyone or do what you do not want to do. "Fuck-you freedom" follows from it and can also come from not relying on other groups. You can travel when and where you like and skip showing up to work on time or at all.

Chris Williamson names a third kind that is cheaper, more common and possibly more effective, the "fuck you family".

Many fathers told him their priorities changed completely when they started a family.

- The status games they had played looked petty, and the effort to impress powerful people looked juvenile and shallow.
- Anxiety about whether people liked them or thought they were cool faded.
- The only people they needed to impress were asleep at home. To their children they were the coolest, richest, strongest, most heroic person on earth.

Williamson suspects that much of what young men do is a surrogate activity until they have a family. Fathers do not all turn placid and ambition does not die. They simply care much less about the minor things that used to fill their lives.
