# Ambition expects you to close the gap and entitlement expects others to

Ambition is expecting yourself to close the gap between what you have and what you want. Entitlement is expecting others to do it for you.

Ambition in this sense means:

- Taking full ownership of your outcomes.
- Not waiting for external validation or assistance.
- Focusing on what you can control.
- Building trust with yourself through action.

A second point is that many ambitions are insecurities, and many lives are driven by shame more than by ambition. It usually takes failure to see the illusions we have been chasing. Williamson puts it as "Some people need to prove to the world that they're worthy."
