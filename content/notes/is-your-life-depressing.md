# Ask whether your life is depressing before asking whether you are depressed

Three questions help tell a personal problem from a reaction to circumstances.

- **Am I depressed or is my life depressing?** This separates clinical depression from situational unhappiness. What feels like depression can be a natural response to difficult circumstances.
- **Am I anxious or would any normal person find my life anxiety-inducing?** This separates an anxiety disorder from a reasonable stress response to a stressful situation.
- **Am I a loner or are the people around me not supportive?** This separates personal social difficulty from an unsupportive environment.

Each one asks you to check the circumstances before assuming the fault lies in you.
