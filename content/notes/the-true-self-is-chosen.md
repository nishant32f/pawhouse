# The true self is projected from your values

On episode 1100 of Modern Wisdom, Chris Williamson questions whether a "true self" exists. People usually treat good changes in someone as the real person showing and bad changes as corruption, trauma or a mask. When judging a conflicted person, they call whichever side matches their own values authentic.

- With allies, virtues are essence and failures are slips.
- With opponents, virtues are performance and failures are revelations.

His conclusion is "The true self isn't discovered. It's basically invented." The "true self" may be a moral projection and lacks an inner essence.

The fiction has uses. It makes forgiveness and love possible. It also lets us excuse our own side too easily, condemn opponents too quickly, and overlook cruelty, group loyalty and self-excusing stories.
