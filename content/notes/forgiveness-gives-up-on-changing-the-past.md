# Forgiveness is giving up on changing the past

A Three Minute Monday newsletter from Chris Williamson carries this line, with no attribution given.

> "Forgiveness is choosing to no longer suffer the things that you cannot change."

Forgiveness does not pretend the injury was fine. It gives up the impossible project of altering history. Clinging to the hope that the past could have been different keeps the wound current. Forgiving lightens the person carrying it, and only sometimes the person forgiven.

The note [[apology-is-not-accountability|An apology is not accountability and forgiveness is not access]] adds that forgiving someone does not mean giving them access to you again.
