# Hormozi's rules for building wealth

Content inspired by Alex Hormozi, who built companies with $100M+ in annual revenue and passed $100M net worth by 31. Owning the outcome ("My fault") is in [[blame-gives-away-power|Blaming something gives it power over you]], focus in [[you-cant-be-everything|You can be anything but not everything]], and the income-to-pain scale in [[excellence-is-capacity-to-take-pain|Excellence is the capacity to take pain]].

## Start with what you have

You have time but lack capital or connections, so offset low leverage with volume. There is no shortcut. Expect a glass period where every step hurts until the terrain turns coarse, then neutral, then sweet.

- **12x30.** Work 12 hours a day for 30 days, no weekends. It shows you are not made of glass, you have another gear, and others outwork you.
- **Volume x leverage = work.** Repetition raises output per effort. A veteran has done it 1,000 times and has no anxiety.
- **20 hours.** Twenty focused hours make you competent. Most people never start.
- **Document, demonstrate, duplicate.** The expert writes out the process, you watch while they explain, then you do it in front of them until it is right.

## Money

- The first $100,000 is the hardest. It took five years, a dollar at a time. Minimum wage will not make you wealthy, but you need capital to bet on yourself.
- When broke, buy discount groceries and Goodwill clothes, attend only free events, and use the time for better jobs, side work or high-value skills.
- Ignorance is the most expensive purchase. At $50,000 a year with a $1,000,000 goal, the missing knowledge costs $950,000 a year.
- Rich people buy time, poor people stuff, ambitious people skills, lazy people distractions.
- Automate investing. Make withdrawals manual and difficult.
- Invest in the "SME 500", yourself. Skill development beats the market's 10%.

## Bets, speed and skills

- With nothing, downside is zero and upside is everything. Take unlimited shots. You win or you learn.
- Success is laying bridge bricks, falling, fixing, continuing. "Overnight success" is years of bricks that finally connect.
- Speed earns money. Move deadlines from end of month to end of week (4x), day (7x), hour (8x+), then now. Do five-minute tasks immediately.
- Big goals face less competition because most people think them unrealistic. A billion-dollar company can be easier to build than a good local restaurant, which needs similar sacrifice with less clear paths.
- **Distraction tax.** Every hour off your one thing goes to something less important than your stated goal.
- **100x focus.** 100x results need 100x focus, and slightly better tactics will not get you there.
- Stack skills. A combination of skills that is too costly to copy protects you, and each skill adds to it.
- Keep a failure resume. Attempts go from unknown optimism to known pessimism to informed action to success. Most quit at known pessimism.
- Master basics first (on time, follow through, do what you said, consistency).
- Learn sales or pay someone who can. Every multi-millionaire and billionaire he knows started in high-volume sales such as door-to-door, cold calling or talking to strangers.

## Product, people and self

- He spent 2 months building good products and 2 years marketing them, and should have spent 2 years building great ones. "$100 Million Offers" had zero paid marketing, sold thousands of copies daily for years and hit #1 in its category. A product that everyone who tries it tells someone about is "too good to fail".
- In a lonely chapter you fit neither old friends nor new ones. Most quit here. Winners focus on winning, losers on winners.
- Remove people who lower your odds of hitting your goals. There are no social obligations, only social consequences.
- Nine partnerships failed before one worked. A partner should clearly bring one thing you lack (money, experience, or time and energy). Insecurity makes people think they need someone, so most fail. Never give equity out of fear.
- "I have demons" or "I self-sabotage" is mystical nonsense, he says. It means insufficient skills for certain conditions, which is learnable. Test a limiting belief with logic (define it in behavior), evidence (what proves it) and utility (does believing it help).

## In 60 seconds

Figure out what you want. Ignore the opinions of others. Do so much volume that it would be unreasonable to not be successful.
