# Work expands to fill the time available

> "Work expands so as to fill the time available for its completion"

This is why assignments were left until the night before and why the cupboard you keep meaning to clean out never gets cleaned. Without a deadline, tasks have no finality and get pushed behind urgent ones that do. With a deadline, tasks wait until the boundary of lateness.

Laura Vanderkam describes a related effect. One widely circulated survey claimed the average social media consumer spends 116 minutes a day on these sites, a category of time that did not exist twenty-five years ago. She asks where the time came from and has no answer.

> "Time stretches to accommodate what people find interesting to do."

Compulsions take up leisure time, and for most people the most compulsive activity is the phone. Studies show perception of time speeds up on social media, so the time is lost and life also goes faster. The writer says he does not want to find out he spent two hours a day speedrunning life at 1.5x.
