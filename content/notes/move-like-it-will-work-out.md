# Moving as if it will work out keeps you moving

Two short quotes in the collection make the same claim, which is that belief comes first.

Ross Harkness says to be delusional, and to believe you can make it work no matter what and that it will all work out in the end. His reasoning is that if you do not believe you have what it takes, your subconscious will do everything it can to prove you right. You do not need to be special, talented or fully supported. You need to believe fully that you can figure it out, and sooner or later you will. Anything else leads to self-sabotage and failure.

Leadersonlyco gives the second version. Mindset is the engine and faith is the fuel. Stop second guessing and show up as if it were already done. Doubt has no place in a vision that big. When you move with belief, doors open, opportunities appear and the right people align. You have survived too much to play it small now. Trust your process, your pace and the payoff, and keep going.

## The disagreement

[[act-before-you-believe|You don't need self-belief before acting]] says the reverse. Belief is optional, and acting first builds the evidence belief later rests on. The two notes disagree on whether belief has to come before action. This note says doubt makes the action fail. The other says people succeed while doubting. Both say to keep going.
