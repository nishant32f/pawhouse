# The last 20 percent of effort carries most of the return

The theory is that 80 percent effort returns 80 percent, and 100 percent effort returns 1,000 percent. The last 20 percent is where the return is, because almost nobody is willing to go all in.

What makes the last stretch possible, in the author's words, is a mix of delusion, obsession and a pinch of childhood trauma. It is the part where you keep going long after logic says retreat. Your biggest weakness becomes your biggest strength, which is an inability to stop. At some point, success depends less on talent or luck than on who will suffer longest. Few people can tolerate the pain this takes.
