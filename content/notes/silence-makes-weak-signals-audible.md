# Silence makes weak signals audible

> "Your mind whispers most of the important insights."

A loud life makes you less sensitive to quiet inner guidance, so you miss it. Sometimes you can detect the useful signal only in stillness.

For people who take in a lot of input, silence is often what is missing. More information is not always the answer. In the 1100th Modern Wisdom episode Chris Williamson says "The answers you seek are in the silence you're avoiding."

The same episode adds a point on load. A hard life does not always need to become easier. Sometimes it needs to become simpler, because many small open loops feel crushing and simplicity restores sequence and traction.
