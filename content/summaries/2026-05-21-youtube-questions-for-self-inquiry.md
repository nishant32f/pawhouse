# Questions for Self-Inquiry

Source: <https://www.youtube.com/shorts/lcfvR4DTPfg>
Summarized: 2026-05-21 22:02 IST
Tool: steipete/summarize

## Brief

- The short is a compact list of journal prompts for separating intrinsic desire from external approval.
- It asks what you would do if "money and status were no object," and what you would say if you were less afraid of other people's opinions.
- It repeatedly pressures the definition of success: first generally, then with money removed, then with money and followers removed.
- The final questions point inward: what are you hiding from yourself, and what emotions are you unprepared to feel?

## Full Summary

The video is not an argument so much as a diagnostic checklist. Its core move is subtraction: remove money, status, followers, and fear of judgment, then ask what remains.

Key prompts:

- "If money and status were no object, what would I do?"
- "If I was less afraid of other people's opinions, what would I say?"
- "What actually makes me happy?"
- "Does success make me happy?"
- "What is my definition of success?"
- "What is my definition of success if money was taken out of the equation?"
- "What is my definition of success if money and followers were taken out of the equation?"
- "What are the things that I'm hiding from myself?"
- "What are the emotions that I'm unprepared to feel?"
