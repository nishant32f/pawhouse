# Boundary scripts

Four short lists on handling difficult people, kept as written. None names an original author.

## 20 types to avoid

Traits that make healthy friendships impossible.

1. Always complaining about everything.
2. Loves drama and thrives in chaos.
3. Only reaches out when they need something.
4. Never takes responsibility for their mistakes.
5. Manipulates situations to get their way.
6. Always makes it about them, never about you.
7. Can't handle your success or happiness.
8. Pretends to be someone they're not.
9. Sees the worst in every situation.
10. Can't keep a secret to save their life.
11. Always has something negative to say about you.
12. Doesn't celebrate your wins, only their own.
13. Bails out when you need them the most.
14. Feels threatened by anyone else's success.
15. Lies about everything, big and small.
16. Doesn't make any effort to stay connected.
17. Constantly plays the victim in every scenario.
18. Judges you for every little thing you do.
19. Always taking, but never giving back.
20. Makes bad choices that hold them back every time.

## Responding to victim mentality

Phrases for keeping a boundary and redirecting the conversation. They pair with trait 17 above.

1. "Let's stay focused on the actual issue."
2. "I'm not here to argue feelings, I'm here to address behavior."
3. "I understand you feel hurt. That doesn't cancel what happened."
4. "We can't solve anything if everything becomes about you."
5. "Being uncomfortable doesn't mean you're being attacked."
6. "You're allowed to feel how you feel. I'm still allowed to set boundaries."
7. "If accountability feels like an attack, there's deeper work to be done."

## Phrases to destroy anyone's ego

Sharp phrases aimed at ego-driven behavior and a heavy need for validation.

- "You bring nothing to the table except drama."
- "You're not as relevant as you think you are."
- "It must be exhausting always needing validation."
- "You're living proof that being loud doesn't make you right."
- "I'm not interested in explaining myself to someone who doesn't listen."
- "Your biggest achievement is pretending to care."
- "It's funny how you think I need your approval."

## Very dangerous mind games

Manipulation tactics. Each is listed with the effect it claims.

1. Give compliments in a way that sounds like an insult. Example: "You're actually smarter than you look." It disorients their ego, and now they chase your approval.
2. Casually call them the wrong name, then correct yourself like it's no big deal. They feel devalued, as if you don't care to remember. Instant ego check, instant psychological edge.
3. Say something cryptic like "People never really know what others are capable of." Then act like you didn't mean anything. It seeds fear and they start watching you differently.
4. Reveal something that seems personal but isn't. They open up more than you did, thinking it's mutual. You gain intel and they gain illusion.
5. When someone confronts you, don't defend yourself. Stare and say "Why are you so emotional about this?" Now they explain their own anger and you have flipped the power.
