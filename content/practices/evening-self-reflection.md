# Evening self-reflection

A daily practice for emotional awareness. Ask these five questions at the end of the day.

1. **What am I really worried about?** Separates real concerns from unnecessary anxiety, and actionable worries from rumination.
2. **What am I presently sad about?** Acknowledges the feeling and prevents suppression.
3. **Who has annoyed me and how?** Processes friction and shows patterns in relationships and triggers.
4. **What does my body want?** Connects mind and body through physical needs and sensations.
5. **What is still lovely?** Builds gratitude, balances a negative focus and notices small joys.
