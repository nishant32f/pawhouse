# He Should've Spoken Up

Source: image/screenshot shared with Hermes on 2026-08-09. The Instagram Reel overlay partly obscured a middle couplet, so the unreadable words are marked below. [[poems-from-motiversity-compilation|Poems from Motiversity Compilation]] also lists this poem as a Motiversity capture, which does not match this source.

> If a man speaks anger,  
> they call him vile.  
>
> If he speaks fear,  
> they call him soft.  
>
> If he speaks boundaries,  
> they call him insecure.  
>
> If he speaks sa[unclear],  
> they call him a [unclear].  
>
> If he speaks nothing,  
> they call him cold.  
>
> If he finally asks for help,  
> they call it weakness.  
>
> And when he disappears under the weight  
> of everything he wasn't allowed to say,  
>
> they whisper,  
>
> “He should've spoken up.”
>
> — tales and ink

## Notes

The poem is about communication, boundaries and vulnerability. The man is criticised for speaking, criticised for staying silent, and then blamed for not speaking sooner.
