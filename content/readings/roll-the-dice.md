# Roll the Dice

*Charles Bukowski*

## Selected excerpt

> If you're going to try, go all the way.  
> Otherwise, don't even start.

> Isolation is the gift.  
> All the others are a test of your endurance,  
> of how much you really want to do it.

> Do it.  
> Do it.  
> Do it all the way.

## Why it matters

The poem argues against half-commitment. It is extreme by design and asks whether the thing is worth losing comfort, approval and ordinary safety.
