# Excerpts from The Seafarer and The Wanderer

*Anonymous Old English poems. The translator is not named.*

## Selected excerpts

> Often I war with waves,  
> battle the winds,  
> strive against both at once,  
> meaning to find the ground wave-covered.  
> Home is a stranger to me.

> A wise person should be patient,  
> should not be too hot-hearted,  
> nor too hasty with words.  
> Neither too weak a warrior  
> nor too reckless.

> A sharp warrior must know the difference  
> between words and deeds.

## Why it matters

The excerpts link hardship to wisdom. They advise patience, restraint in speech, and knowing the difference between words and deeds.
