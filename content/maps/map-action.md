# Action and discipline

How to start, how to keep going, and what to give up to do either.

## Starting

- [[act-before-you-believe|You don't need self-belief before acting]]. Acting without self-belief generates the evidence that belief later rests on, and waiting for the right internal state is a way to avoid acting.
- [[move-like-it-will-work-out|Moving as if it will work out keeps you moving]]. Two short quotes argue that believing it will work out is the precondition for making it work, and that doubt leads to self-sabotage.
- [[preparation-becomes-avoidance|Preparation becomes avoidance once it delays execution]]. Preparation helps only while it increases execution. After that it protects you from being seen failing, and the next physical step is the cure.
- [[self-awareness-can-stall-action|Self-awareness can simulate futures faster than action resolves them]]. Reflection lets you pre-live rejection and failure before anything happens, so courage for overthinkers means moving while things are unclear.
- [[omission-errors-cost-more|Errors of omission cost more than they feel]]. Mistakes of doing show up fast, mistakes of not doing leave no evidence, so the cost of inaction has to be counted on purpose.
- [[everyone-is-rooting-for-you|Most people are rooting for you]]. People entering an interaction usually hope you succeed because your success validates their judgment, which removes the story that the deck was stacked against you.

## Keeping going

- [[consistency-is-a-competitive-edge|Consistency is rare enough to be an edge]]. When most people avoid discomfort, modest consistency, intentionality and resilience already put you ahead, with limits on how much weight to carry for others.
- [[self-trust-from-kept-promises|Self-trust is built by keeping small promises]]. Confidence comes from completed commitments to yourself, so start with promises small enough to keep and finish them fully.
- [[obsession-inverts-friction|Obsession removes the friction from hard work]]. Obsession removes the friction that discipline pushes through, cannot be summoned, and fades, so use it to build routines while it lasts.
- [[the-last-twenty-percent|The last 20 percent of effort carries most of the return]]. A stated theory that 100 percent effort returns far more than 80 percent, because few people are willing to keep going past the point where logic says stop.
- [[monk-mode-needs-an-end-date|Monk mode needs an end date and a way back]]. Isolation can produce focused growth but can also turn retreat into identity, so set a deadline and return to the world.
- [[perseverance-under-pressure|Perseverance is keeping your behaviour intact after failure]]. High achievers make frequent mistakes under pressure, so the skill that counts is recovering quickly and treating setbacks as information.

## Choosing what to pursue

- [[you-cant-be-everything|You can be anything but not everything]]. You can change direction far more than you believe, but time is finite, so progress requires narrow and deep focus and deciding what to say no to.
- [[want-the-lifestyle-not-just-the-outcome|Wanting the outcome without its lifestyle guarantees disappointment]]. Some people want success they lack the resilience or work ethic to reach, and the choices are to do the work, drop the aim, or stay stuck.
- [[comfort-over-success|Many people want comfort more than success]]. A 2017 forum comment argues that many people do not want success, and that self-defeating beliefs may protect them from blame for failing.
- [[opportunities-expire-with-life-stage|Some opportunities expire with your life stage]]. Some extreme efforts are only reasonable at certain ages, and later years trade physical and time freedom for financial freedom.
- [[crisis-shows-what-youre-made-of|Only a crisis shows what you are made of]]. Most hard things in life are chosen, so only an unchosen worst case shows whether your best self can show up.
- [[goals-are-hypotheses|Treat goals as hypotheses to test]]. A goal held as a contract to self-criticise until it is met turns ambition into a threat, while a hypothesis can fail without harm.
