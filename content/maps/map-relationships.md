# Relationships

Partners, family, friends and the boundaries that protect them.

## Partners

- [[relationships-are-decided-in-the-lows|Relationships are decided in the lows]]. How a couple handles bad times predicts the relationship better than how they enjoy good times, so a partner should be chosen with that in mind.
- [[marriage-as-conversation|Choose a partner by the quality of conversation]]. Nietzsche's test for marriage is whether you can keep having good conversations with this person into old age.
- [[darwin-marriage-decision|Darwin Marriage Decision]]. Charles Darwin's written pros and cons list on whether to marry, with his conclusion.
- [[feynmans-love-letter|Feynman's Love Letter]]. A letter Richard Feynman wrote to his wife D'Arline two years after her death.

## Family

- [[family-frees-you-from-status-games|A family frees you from status games]]. Fathers report that starting a family ended their need to impress status holders, because the people who matter most are at home.
- [[modern-fatherhood|Present fathers are more exhausted and more fulfilled]]. Modern fathers spend far more time on childcare than earlier generations and report more fatigue and more satisfaction with life.

## Friends

- [[real-friends|Real friends are the people you filter yourself least around]]. Ask who you filter yourself least around and who you can sit in silence with, then spend your time with them.
- [[text-your-friends|Text a friend when they cross your mind]]. When a friend crosses your mind, send a short message, since friendships fade from drift more than from conflict.
- [[reverse-charisma|Good company makes you feel interesting]]. The people we like most make us feel interesting, which depends on how much of us they can take in without our editing.
- [[supporting-men|Men need to hear both \"you can be more\" and \"you are enough\"]]. Men want inspiration and acceptance together, and the hard part is offering support that does not make them feel broken or insufficient.

## Boundaries

- [[apology-is-not-accountability|An apology is not accountability and forgiveness is not access]]. Regret and good words do not show a pattern has changed, so trust follows behaviour and forgiveness need not restore access.
- [[boundary-scripts|Boundary scripts]]. Four lists for dealing with difficult people. Traits to avoid, replies to victim mentality, ego-checking phrases and manipulation tactics.
- [[toxic-compassion|Compassion that avoids truth does harm]]. Putting short-term emotional comfort ahead of reality and long-term outcomes optimises for looking good rather than doing good.
