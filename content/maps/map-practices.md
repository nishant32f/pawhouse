# Practices

Protocols, question lists, scripts and templates meant to be used.

## Practices

- [[boundary-scripts|Boundary scripts]]. Four lists for dealing with difficult people. Traits to avoid, replies to victim mentality, ego-checking phrases and manipulation tactics.
- [[evening-self-reflection|Evening self-reflection]]. Five questions to ask each evening to process worries, sadness, irritation, bodily needs and what is still good.
- [[huberman-protocols|Huberman protocols]]. A simplified list of practical protocols drawn from Huberman Lab episodes, covering sleep, caffeine, cold, heat, fitness, focus, learning and supplements.
- [[plan-your-new-era|Plan your new era]]. Twenty-five questions for reflecting on your current state and planning goals for the year ahead.
- [[questions-to-ask-yourself|Questions to ask yourself]]. Journal prompts from Morgan Housel's list, shared by Chris Williamson, plus a short set that removes money, status and followers one at a time.
- [[resignation-retention-letter|Resignation retention letter]]. A letter pattern for responding to a valued teammate's resignation with warmth and a real invitation to stay.
