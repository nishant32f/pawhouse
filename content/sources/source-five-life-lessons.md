# Five life lessons

Five short lessons credited to Simon Sinek, posted from Chris Williamson's Modern Wisdom podcast.

## Concept notes

- [[blame-gives-away-power|Blaming something gives it power over you]]. Lesson 2, sometimes you are the problem, and you can claim credit for what you do right as long as you take responsibility for what you do wrong.
- [[speak-last|Speak last so people feel heard and you decide with more information]]. Lesson 4.
- [[the-position-is-not-you|Better treatment from a position belongs to the position]]. Lesson 5.

## Other points

- Lesson 1. You can go after whatever you want, provided you do not deny anyone else the same. You do not have to do it the way everybody has done it.
- Lesson 3. Take care of each other. To lead, you must be very good at helping the person to your left and the person to your right.
