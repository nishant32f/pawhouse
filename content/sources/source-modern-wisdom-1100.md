# Modern Wisdom 1100, 19 lessons

This is Chris Williamson's Modern Wisdom episode #1100. It reviews recurring lessons and the failure mode inside traits usually treated as pure goods. The summary was made on 2026-05-23 from the YouTube transcript (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RBZTLptAMao).

## Concept notes

- [[obsession-inverts-friction|Obsession removes the friction from hard work]]. Discipline accepts friction, motivation reduces it, obsession inverts it.
- [[self-awareness-can-stall-action|Self-awareness can simulate futures faster than action resolves them]]. Hamlet and the overthinker.
- [[omission-errors-cost-more|Errors of omission cost more than they feel]]. Front-load the pain of inaction.
- [[monk-mode-needs-an-end-date|Monk mode needs an end date and a way back]]. Integration as the fourth I.
- [[carrying-everything-becomes-self-abandonment|Carrying everyone else's load becomes self-abandonment]]. Strength in work becomes harm in relationships.
- [[silence-makes-weak-signals-audible|Silence makes weak signals audible]]. More information is not the answer.
- [[the-true-self-is-chosen|The true self is projected from your values]]. Authenticity follows the values we approve.
- [[familiar-hell-vs-unfamiliar-heaven|The nervous system prefers a familiar hell to an unfamiliar heaven]]. Source of the familiar misery quote.

## Other points

- Hard periods work as workload exposure therapy. Once you survive a level of demand, your body learns it has been there before.
- A hard life does not need to be easier but simpler. Many small open loops feel crushing, and simplicity restores sequence and traction.
- Sex-difference findings named in the episode. Men often overestimate female friends' attraction to them. Men may rely more on romantic relationships for social support. Couples often settle at a sexual frequency closer to the lower-desire partner.
- Stereotypes sometimes invert reality, and social scripts hide asymmetric costs.
- Polyamory suits a small minority of exceptional communicators with stable nervous systems. Many others mistake appetite, avoidance or spiritual bypassing for emotional mastery. The existence of rare people does not show you are built for it.
