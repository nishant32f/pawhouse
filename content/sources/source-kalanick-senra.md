# Travis Kalanick with David Senra

David Senra's YouTube interview with Travis Kalanick, summarized 2026-08-25. Link https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QVnU5lGlKE8.

## Concept notes

- [[problem-solving-capacity|Problem-solving capacity must keep up with problem creation]]. Meta problem, super pumped, fewest rules, Uber pricing approval.
- [[excellence-is-capacity-to-take-pain|Excellence is the capacity to take pain]]. Marathoner, numbness, fierceness with calm.

## Other points

- In China, Uber had to relearn maps, regulation and partnerships. A home playbook is a liability until relearned locally.
- Ride sharing is a network-effect business. Against Didi, which copied fast, subsidies funded the fight until efficiency could outstrip them.
- Kalanick says progress in China had to be in harmony with stability. Uber gave a local partner a smaller stake than the half others advised. Chinese sovereign wealth funds later backed Uber's rivals elsewhere.
- He calls New York's taxi medallions a captured license system. Anti-capitalism blocks people from entering a vocation or customers from choosing. "The one who serves the customer best gets all the customers."
- He says 2010s media narratives about companies turned into political mudslinging.
- On Benchmark and his removal, he says investors without operating context can do damage. The bar for a venture investor is "do no harm".
- Fundraising is about process more than valuation. Tell a numbers-backed story until it reads as proven, then build the demand curve and clear the market.
- He ran Uber too close to the line. At $70 billion, staying technically in bounds was not enough.
- Adams builds specialized physical AI one industry at a time. Humanoids suit low-scale home tasks, while a facility making 1,000 pancakes an hour wants a purpose-built machine. Early areas are food, mining and "wheelbase for robots".
