# Alain de Botton: you'll never feel enough until you understand this

Interview with Alain de Botton on the Nuvola YouTube channel, 15:10 long, covering what drives ambition and fame and how to find a life that fits you.

## Concept notes

- [[ambition-can-be-insecurity|Outsized ambition is often powered by an outsized wound]]. Success and fame as compensation for unhappiness, invisibility and lack of love.
- [[envy-is-a-signal|Envy points at capabilities you may have]]. Using envy and a 12 scenario exercise to find your direction.
- [[make-peace-with-your-averageness|Happiness is achieving what you are capable of]]. Tolerating averageness, and accepting the feelings you resist.

## Other points

- De Botton quotes the School of Life view that a well-parented child has no wish to be famous.
- He says the interview exists because something was bothering him, since a fully happy person would have declined the invitation.
