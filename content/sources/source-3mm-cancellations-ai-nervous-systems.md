# 3MM: cancellations, AI and nervous systems

A Three Minute Monday newsletter by Chris Williamson (Modern Wisdom). The vault copy carries no date or link.

## Concept notes

- [[familiar-hell-vs-unfamiliar-heaven|The nervous system prefers a familiar hell to an unfamiliar heaven]]. Kathy Overman's line on repeated relational patterns.

## Other points

- Chris separates moral condemnation from status collapse. A cancellation says "here is a bad person", and a cringe cancellation says "here is an uncool person". Consequences speed up when the crowd can make both judgments at once.
- He quotes a line that momentum is cancellation's condom (see [[reputation-momentum|Reputation holds while momentum looks like approval]]).
- AI exposure by sex. The newsletter quotes "Women make up 47% of the total workforce, but they comprise 83% of people employed in artificial intelligence-vulnerable occupations." The newsletter names no reporter, date or study. Chris treats it as a likely culture-war flashpoint, because uneven pain can be read as identity-based injustice.
- Chris draws a general point from the Denzel Rust quote. Most people want the person in front of them to turn out well because it reflects on their own judgment.
