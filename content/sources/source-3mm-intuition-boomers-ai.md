# 3MM: intuition, boomers and AI

Chris Williamson's Three Minute Monday email, kept here as a list of the ideas it carried.

## Concept notes

- [[rules-before-exceptions|Intuition is earned by following the rules first]]. Oliver Wendell Holmes Jr. says the young man knows the rules and the old man knows the exceptions.
- [[marriage-as-conversation|Choose a partner by the quality of conversation]]. Nietzsche's test for marriage.
- [[text-your-friends|Text a friend when they cross your mind]]. Small messages keep a friendship alive.
- [[modern-fatherhood|Present fathers are more exhausted and more fulfilled]]. Derek Thompson on childcare time across generations.

## Other points

- Williamson's takeaways were to earn the right to improvise by mastering fundamentals, to lower the bar for keeping friendships warm, and to treat a meaningful load as different from a broken life.
