# End of year lessons, 2025

A 2025 list of lessons. The list closes with a credit to Chris Williamson's Modern Wisdom podcast. Each section is now its own note.

## Concept notes

- [[strengths-and-wounds-are-one-metal|Your strengths and your wounds come from the same source]]. We blame parents for flaws and do not credit them for strengths.
- [[advice-amplifies-what-you-are|Advice amplifies what you already are]]. Advice hyperresponders.
- [[vulnerability-is-not-weakness|Restraint is not the same as resilience]]. Toxic stoicism and intimacy.
- [[preparation-becomes-avoidance|Preparation becomes avoidance once it delays execution]]. Procrastination as fear of being seen failing.
- [[relationships-are-decided-in-the-lows|Relationships are decided in the lows]]. Eight red flags and partner traits.
- [[self-worth-beyond-validation|Self-worth outsourced to approval is never secure]]. Neediness.
- [[the-shame-of-small-fears|Being ashamed of small fears makes them worse]]. Old nervous system, new threats.
- [[carrying-everything-becomes-self-abandonment|Carrying everyone else's load becomes self-abandonment]]. The Atlas complex.

## Other points

- Victor Hugo finished *The Hunchback of Notre Dame* by locking away all his clothes except a wool shawl, buying a large bottle of ink and staying indoors.
- Productivity has three levels. Inputs are effort applied, outputs are work done, outcomes are real results. Measuring inputs makes you good at trying, outputs at producing, outcomes at winning. The test is whether this moved you closer to your goals.
