## Other points

- Employment statistic. Chris cites George TheTinMen quoting the US Labor Dept. Of 369,000 jobs created "since last year", 348,000 went to women and 21,000 to men, which is 94% of net US employment growth. The newsletter gives no date for the period. Chris's reading is that demographic shifts quickly become identity stories.
- Oliver Burkeman, quoted in the newsletter, says to pour finite time and energy into something more absorbing than trying to control life, which is living it. It sits in [[worry-gets-false-credit|Worry takes credit for results it did not produce]].
- The breathwork anecdote. Chris made himself pass out on breath holds because if two minutes was good, three must be better.
