# 3MM: meditation, criminals and politics

A Three Minute Monday newsletter by Chris Williamson (Modern Wisdom). The vault copy carries no date or link.

## Concept notes

- [[goals-are-hypotheses|Treat goals as hypotheses to test]]. Goals as contracts to self-criticise until you succeed.
- [[forgiveness-gives-up-on-changing-the-past|Forgiveness is giving up on changing the past]]. Forgiving means no longer suffering what you cannot change.
- [[healing-needs-safety-and-feeling|Healing needs safety plus contact with emotion]]. Also detachment numbing reward, resentment as a guard dog, and anger turning into sadness.
- [[silence-makes-weak-signals-audible|Silence makes weak signals audible]]. "Your mind whispers most of the important insights."
- [[marriage-as-conversation|Choose a partner by the quality of conversation]]. The heart decides and the head makes it happen.

## Other points

- "As you bring more awareness to yourself, it becomes harder to not be yourself." Misalignment gets harder to ignore once you notice it. See [[self-worth-beyond-validation|Self-worth outsourced to approval is never secure]].
- Steve Stewart-Williams, quoted via Rob Henderson, says research finds men get longer sentences than women for the same crime, after accounting for criminal history, offence type, seriousness, marital status and childcare responsibilities. The note gives no year or study.
- Jack Butcher says moving from theory to practice costs status. Criticising is cheap because it keeps distance, and building exposes you to visible error.
- @mmay3r says politics is so corrosive that people want things to go badly for everyone when their opponents are in power.
- The newsletter's life hack is to call your mum today.
