Machine Herald newspaper layout

Approved on 21 October 2026 after the user viewed the desktop/mobile newspaper preview and said “I like this one, go with it”.

Goal capsule

Apply the approved compact masthead, ruled columns and readable phone layout to the actual request-time reader. Preserve the separation between accepted content, publishing and presentation.

Requirements

  1. Use the approved palette and existing Old Standard TT/Newsreader fonts, compact left-aligned desktop masthead, date/count cell and an Inside list derived from the selected edition.
  2. Arrange the lead and secondary articles in the main column and actual brief stories in a distinct ruled sidebar. On phones stack articles and briefs with readable text, no horizontal overflow and no oversized desktop canvas.
  3. Show bounded front-page previews without rewriting authored prose. Full stories, sources and numerical qualifiers remain available at their existing canonical article and full-edition routes.
  4. Keep all active stories exactly once on the front page. Preserve persistent and legacy anchors, archived access, single-story and legacy editions, and work without JavaScript.
  5. Preserve authored image/chart rendering and their sources. Do not hardcode the one-off DeepInfer drawing or today's facts into the general reader.
  6. Retain theme and text-size controls, archive/search, RSS discovery and existing dated/canonical URLs.
  7. Do not change the neutral compiler, R2 schema, publisher, accepted Markdown, Hermes configuration, cron, Telegram, main Quartz website or RSS bytes.

Settled decisions

The reference and approved 21 October preview settle the visual direction. The preview is retained in local branch preview/newspaper-20261021, commit b0ddb3d8. Production keeps canonical article navigation rather than importing the preview-only device controls or frozen content.
